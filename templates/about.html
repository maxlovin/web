About Me

This is what I do.

Fitness isn't about sitting on a machine moving back and forth in a single plane—it’s about absolute physical mastery. My approach to training sits at the intersection of bodyweight mastery, explosive spatial movement, and real-world combat readiness.

Calisthenics & Movement Control: Mastering leverage, relative strength, and strict body control. From weighted pull-ups to static holds, it’s about building a frame that moves effortlessly relative to its own weight.

Parkour & Spatial Awareness: Adapting to any environment. Vaulting, jumping, and momentum management turn the urban landscape into a canvas, training split-second decision-making, fast footwork, and high-impact resilience.

MMA & Combat Readiness: The ultimate trial of durability, endurance, and strategy. Blending striking, wrestling, and ground control requires explosive rotational power, rapid reflexes, and absolute focus under pressure.
